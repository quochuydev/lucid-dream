# ⚔️ The Kingdom Beyond the Mist – Song of the Last Knight

Read slowly. Don't rush. Imagine that you are the main character of this story.

That night, snow was falling on the roof.

You are lying in a small room. Outside the window stands a pine forest, dark and deep. The north wind moves through the treetops, carrying a sound like the flutes of ancient spirits.

You close your eyes.

When you open them, you are no longer in the room.

You are standing in the middle of a snow-covered forest.

Before you lies an old cobblestone road. Along both sides, blue torches burn, though no one has lit them. In the distance, an enormous castle sits atop a mountain, its tall towers piercing the clouds.

A black raven is perched on a branch.

It looks at you and speaks in a deep, rasping voice:

"At last, the chosen one has come."

You feel no fear. Strangely, everything here seems familiar, as if you had been here long ago in a dream.

The raven flaps its wings.

A wooden chest appears on the snow.

Inside are a suit of silver armor, a deep blue cloak, and a sword whose hilt is carved with a northern dragon.

As you touch the sword, a voice echoes in your mind:

"You did not find the sword. The sword has been waiting for you."

You put on the cloak, fasten the sword at your side, and walk on.

The road leads you to an old village.

Wooden houses with snow-covered roofs stand close together. Smoke rises from the chimneys. Golden light spills from the windows. In the tavern, dwarves clink their mugs of ale, and Viking warriors tell tales of sea dragons and of gods who have fallen asleep.

An old man in a fur coat walks up to you.

He looks at the sword at your side, then bows his head.

"Sir Knight, the North is waiting for you."

He tells you that the kingdom of Eldoria has been buried in winter for a hundred years. The ice dragon Frostheim sleeps on the peak at the very end of the world. As long as it lives, spring will never return.

But he does not ask you to fight right away.

He only smiles and says:

"Rest tonight. Tomorrow, your adventure truly begins."

You step into the room on the top floor of the inn.

A large bed draped in furs rests beside the fireplace. Outside the window, snow falls silently on the rooftops.

You take off your armor, lay the sword beside the bed, and lie down.

Just before you drift off to sleep, you hear a bell ringing from the distant castle.

One toll.

Two tolls.

Three tolls.

As the fourth toll rings out, you open your eyes.

You are standing on the balcony of a castle.

Beneath your feet lies a vast kingdom.

Pine forests stretch all the way to the horizon. Snow-capped mountains reflect the moonlight. A silver river winds through the valley. Far away, dragons fly among the peaks, while a blue-violet aurora dances across the sky.

A white horse with a silver mane is waiting for you in the courtyard.

Beside it lies an ancient map.

The map shows countless places:

- The Forest of the Sleeping Fairies.
- The Golden City of the Dwarves.
- The Frozen Sea where the dragons live.
- The Valley of the Giants.
- The Last Castle of the Northern Gods.

You realize something.

This is not a story you are reading.

This is your world.

You can choose any path. You can become a knight, an explorer, a king, or a wanderer who belongs to no kingdom.

No one decides for you.

You reach out and touch the sword at your side.

It glows a pale blue.

And suddenly you realize...

You are dreaming.

You smile.

Because tonight, an entire world is waiting for you to explore it.

The north wind blows past.

The white horse neighs loudly.

And the adventure of the last knight officially begins.

## 🌌 Before You Sleep

After reading, close your eyes and imagine these 3 images:

1. You stand in a snow-covered pine forest, wearing a knight's cloak.
2. You hold a silver sword and see the castle on the mountaintop.
3. You ride a white horse toward the aurora.

Repeat gently in your mind:

"When I enter that world, I will realize I am dreaming."

Don't try to force the dream to appear. Let yourself sink into sleep, carrying with you the image of the kingdom of Eldoria.

May you have a wonderful adventure in the North, brave knight. ⚔️❄️
